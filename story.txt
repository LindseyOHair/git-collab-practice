Once upon a time there was a witch who lived at the top of a mountain. She hated everyone and loved playing tricks on the town. One day, she was getting food and the kids decided to get revenge and steal what she was eating.
The witch quickly realized that the children had stolen her food and became furious. She decided to follow them back to town and teach them a lesson they would never forget.
She invited each of them back to her house and fed them. When they asked to leave at the end of the night, she said no and turned them into kitchen appliances.
The End
